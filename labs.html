CISA Audit Lab

Eight hands-on labs that put you in the auditor’s seat with realistic evidence and working tools. Take a ticket, test the evidence, record verified exceptions, write the finding, and print a graded lab report.

Labs: 8Each: 45 to 90 minScore: 100 pointsMode: Individual or teamReports: Print or PDF

How the lab works

Real audit work on simulated evidence

Take the ticket

Each lab opens with an engagement ticket from the audit director and a 100-point checklist.

Use the tools

SQL console, report viewer, test application, evidence workbench, recovery clock, or audit console. All run in the browser.

Verify and debrief

Check each item as often as needed. Reveal the auditor’s debrief after a first try, at the cost of that item’s points.

Lab report

Print or save a graded report with answers, the written finding, team members, and a signature line.

For faculty

Using the Audit Lab in class

  • As submissions: ask learners or teams to print the lab report to PDF and upload it. Reports show the score out of 100, attempts per item, recorded answers, the written deliverable, and which debriefs were revealed.
  • Team mode: one learner drives the tool while the team decides, then the team submits one report listing every member.
  • Lab B5 tabletop: open the lab on the projector and choose Facilitator. Teams choose Team and enter the session code. When you pause or release an inject early, a new code appears; teams re-enter it to stay in sync.
  • Before class: Lab B1 loads its SQL engine from the internet, so confirm the classroom network allows it. The other labs run offline.
